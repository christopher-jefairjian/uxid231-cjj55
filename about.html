About Me

My Background

I'm a fourth-year Entrepreneurship student at Drexel University. I've always been interested in business and in understanding how ideas can be developed into products and services that solve real problems.

My experience has taken me through several different industries, including financial services, restaurants, food production, and product development. These experiences have helped shape my interest in entrepreneurship and have given me opportunities to learn through hands-on experience.

Career and Entrepreneurship

After graduating from Drexel, I hope to work in the credit and debt counseling industry as a debt counselor. My previous experience working for a credit counseling company introduced me to the industry and showed me how valuable financial guidance can be for people working to improve their financial situations.

At the same time, I plan to continue developing my own companies and exploring new business opportunities. I'm currently involved in projects in credit counseling, human-grade dog food, and frozen pizza dough. Each project has allowed me to work on different problems and learn more about building and developing a business.

You can learn more about these businesses on my Projects page.

Community Involvement

I've also had the opportunity to work on food donation and volunteer programs throughout the Philadelphia area. While working with Clemens Food Group, I helped identify organizations that could accept large food donations and connect those donations with communities where they could be put to use.

At Ronald McDonald House Charities, I helped coordinate volunteer dinner programs for families and children staying there. I worked alongside hundreds of volunteers and assisted with preparing and serving dinners that fed hundreds of people in a night. These experiences showed me how businesses, organizations, and volunteers can work together to make a meaningful impact in their communities.

Outside of School and Work

Food has always been a big part of my life. I've worked in restaurants for much of my life and enjoy both cooking and baking. Over the years, I've become a skilled baker, and my experience with food has also influenced several of the business projects I'm involved in today.

Outside of business and school, I enjoy exercising, playing basketball, spending time outdoors, and traveling to new places. I also enjoy reading, especially books about finance, business, personal development, and self-improvement.
