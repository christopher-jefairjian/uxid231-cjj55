Hi, I'm Christopher Jefairjian

I am a student at Drexel University studying Entrepreneurship. On this page you will find information about me, my projects, and how to contact me.

What I'm working on

I'm building Credit Catapult, a platform that helps people improve their credit scores and achieve their financial goals. I'm taking this class to learn the fundamentals of UI/UX design so I can apply them to that project.

I also work on a human-grade dog food company that uses retort packaging, which seals and heat-sterilizes food in its pouch so it stays fresh at room temperature for extended periods. The idea came from my work with Ritika's Global Grains, which uses the same technology to preserve cooked rice. Through that work, we've partnered with major retailers on private-label products expected to reach shelves within the next year. Our goal is to give pet owners a more convenient option and give dogs healthier, better-tasting food.

Community Work

I've run food-related volunteer programs for nonprofits across the Philadelphia area, including Ronald McDonald House Charities. Through that work, I've helped donate over 250,000 pounds of meat products to people in need across America, and I've had the opportunity to collaborate with players from the Eagles and the Phillies.

Outside of School

When I'm not working on school or business projects, I enjoy reading, cooking, exercising, spending time outside, and playing sports, especially basketball. I also enjoy traveling and exploring new places, and I try to take at least one trip each year to a new city or country.
