Hi, I'm Christopher Jefairjian

I'm a fourth-year Entrepreneurship student at Drexel University with an interest in building businesses that solve practical problems. My experience spans financial services, food product development, and community-focused work.

After Drexel, I hope to work in the credit and debt counseling industry while continuing to develop the companies and projects I'm involved in.

What I'm Working On

Credit Catapult

I'm currently developing Credit Catapult, a platform focused on making credit counseling services more accessible and scalable. The idea grew from my experience working in the credit counseling industry and seeing opportunities to improve how people access support with credit and debt.

I'm building the platform alongside a computer science student with the goal of combining technology with practical financial guidance.

Food Entrepreneurship

I'm also involved in two food businesses: a human-grade dog food company and a B2B frozen pizza dough company. My work has given me hands-on experience with product development, food production, and bringing ideas from experimentation toward commercial products.

Visit my Projects page to learn more about what I'm working on.

Community Involvement

Giving back has also been an important part of my experience. I've helped connect large food donations with organizations that could put them to use and have coordinated volunteer dinner programs for families staying at Ronald McDonald House Charities.

You can learn more about my background on my About page.
