Projects

My entrepreneurial experience has given me the opportunity to work on projects in both financial services and the food industry. Each project has allowed me to work on different problems and gain hands-on experience developing products and businesses.

Credit Catapult

Credit Catapult is a platform I'm developing to make credit counseling services more accessible and scalable. The idea came from my experience working for a credit counseling company, where I became interested in finding better ways to connect people struggling with credit and debt to useful financial guidance.

I'm currently developing Credit Catapult alongside a computer science student and close friend. Our goal is to use technology to create a more convenient way for people to access credit counseling services while still focusing on the individual financial challenges they face.

Human-Grade Dog Food

I'm a 25% owner of a human-grade dog food company where I primarily lead research and development. The company is developing prepared dog food using retort packaging, a process that allows food to remain shelf-stable for extended periods without requiring refrigeration or freezing.

My role focuses on researching, developing, and testing the product. The idea was influenced by my previous experience with Ritika's Global Grains, where I was introduced to the use of retort packaging for shelf-stable food products.

Frozen Pizza Dough

I'm also a 25% owner of a B2B frozen pizza dough company that supplies dough to pizza shops across the country. The goal is to help restaurants simplify their operations by providing a consistent, high-quality dough product that is ready for use.

I assisted in developing and refining the pizza dough recipe, combining my experience working in restaurants with my interest in baking and food product development. Working on the company has given me experience with product development as well as the challenges involved in producing and distributing a food product for other businesses.

Ritika's Global Grains

I previously worked with Ritika's Global Grains, a company that produces retort-packaged rice products primarily for private-label supermarket brands across the country. My experience there introduced me to retort packaging and large-scale food production.

That experience helped develop my interest in food product development and later influenced the idea of applying similar packaging technology to human-grade dog food.
